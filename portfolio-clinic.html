Demo Clinic Website

A clinic website demo with services, doctor profiles and an appointment form.

View the live demo

This demo shows a calm, clear clinic website. Patients can read about treatments, meet the doctors on profile cards and send an appointment request with a simple form. Clear pages and an easy booking route are what patients look for first.

A real clinic site of this type would carry the clinic's own services, doctors, timings and location. This is a demo website, made to show the design and how the pages work.

What this demo includes

  • Home page with a clear appointment button
  • Service pages that explain each treatment in simple words
  • Doctor profile cards with photo areas and roles
  • Appointment request form with date and service fields
  • Opening hours and location block
  • Layout that stays clear and easy on phones
